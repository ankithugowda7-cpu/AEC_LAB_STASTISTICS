Binomial PMF Calculator: $P(X = k)$

Compute point probability mass function for exact target success count $k$.

$X \sim \text{Binomial}(n, p)$
Range: $0 \le k \le 20$
PMF Formula Definition
$$P(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} \, p^k \, (1-p)^{n-k}$$

Where $\binom{n}{k} = C(n,k) = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}$ represents combinations of selecting $k$ successes from $n$ trials.

Calculated Point Probability
P(X = 7) = 0.1844
Percentage 18.44%
Step-by-Step Combination & Factor Breakdown
1. Combinations $C(n,k)$ 77,520
2. Success factor $p^k$ 0.000643
3. Failure factor $(1-p)^{n-k}$ 0.003697
Full Expression Evaluation P(X=7) = 77,520 × (0.35)^7 × (0.65)^13 = 0.184378
Contextual PMF Interpretation:

This represents the exact probability of obtaining exactly 7 successes out of 20 independent trials.

Binomial CDF Calculator: $P(X \le k)$ & $P(X > k)$

Compute cumulative probability functions and complementary upper-tail values.

Cumulative Distribution Function
Cumulative Probability $P(X \le k)$ 60.10%
P(X ≤ 7) = 0.6010
Formula Summation:
$$P(X \le k) = \sum_{j=0}^{k} P(X = j)$$
P(X ≤ 7) means the probability of getting 7 or fewer successes.
Complementary Upper Tail $P(X > k)$ 39.90%
P(X > 7) = 0.3990
Complement Formula:
$$P(X > k) = 1 - P(X \le k)$$
P(X > 7) represents the probability of getting strictly more than 7 successes.
